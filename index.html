ℹ️ How It Works

Crypto Asset Depletion Method

This calculation method is used by mortgage lenders to qualify borrowers who have cryptocurrency assets:

  1. 50% Haircut: Your crypto value is reduced by 50% to account for volatility
  2. 84-Month Depletion: The remaining value is divided by 84 months (7 years)
  3. Qualifying Income: This monthly amount counts as income for mortgage qualification
  4. Affordability: Combined with your other income, we calculate how much home you can afford based on DTI limits
